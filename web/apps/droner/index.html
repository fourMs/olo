Droner (Dan Trueman, 2007)

Turn Audio on, then Space to fade the drone in/out.

Keys 1–8 choose the fundamental. Move the pointer on the stage to crossfade four overtone corners (center = equal mix).

Vertical position on the stage controls volume (replaces laptop tilt). Horizontal adds slight pitch bend.

Space = fade · 1–8 = pitch · drag stage = mix
