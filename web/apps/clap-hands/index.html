Clap Hands (Alexander Refsum Jensenius, 2008)

Turn Audio on and allow the microphone. Clap, snap, or speak — transients feed a cascade of stereo delays (inspired by the Max clap-hands patch).

Adjust feedback and delay time. Works best with laptop speakers at moderate volume to avoid feedback.
