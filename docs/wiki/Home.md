## What is OLO?

Oslo Laptop Orchestra (OLO) is an ad-hoc ensemble of students and faculty from the University of Oslo and the Norwegian Academy of Music. The main idea is to **explore the laptop as a musical instrument** in a collaborative setting. The orchestra performs the standard repertoire for laptop orchestras and develops its own pieces for different setups and constellations.

OLO follows the tradition of [PLOrk](https://plork.cs.princeton.edu/), where each performer has a laptop, speakers, and controllers.

## The three rules

- We think of our laptops as **any other type of instrument**, and perform with them as if we had been playing clarinets, trumpets, etc.
- We embrace the **new possibilities** that digital instruments allow for, and the **social aspects** of playing together.
- We only rely on **sound coming from the extended instrument**; we never use an external PA system.

These rules can be seen as limitations, but they are also liberating and give the ensemble a clear aesthetic, visual, and musical appearance.

## In every web app

- **Learn** — how to play this instrument
- **Audio on/off** — unlock the Web Audio context (required in some browsers until the first click)
- **Volume** — master level (50–150%)
