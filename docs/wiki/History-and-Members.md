# History and members

Source: [RITMO — Oslo Laptop Orchestra](https://www.uio.no/ritmo/english/research/labs/fourms/research/projects/olo/) (updated June 2026).

## Timeline

| Year | Event |
|------|--------|
| **2007** | OLO founded by **Kjell Tore Innervik** and **Alexander Refsum Jensenius** |
| 2007–2009 | Core repertoire built in ChucK and Max/MSP; ties to PLOrk and SMELT |
| 2009+ | Workshops and concerts at irregular intervals |
| **2020s** | Archive consolidated on GitHub; SVN metadata removed |
| **2026** | **`web/`** browser instruments; GitHub Pages; wiki |

OLO was started in the spirit of **PLOrk** (Princeton Laptop Orchestra): each performer with a laptop, speakers, and controllers — exploring collaborative laptop musicking without a central PA.

## Concept (official)

1. Laptops are **instruments**, performed with the same care as acoustic sections.
2. **Digital possibilities** and **social playing** are embraced.
3. **Only the extended instrument speaks** — no external amplification.

## Equipment

Regular laptops with **active studio speakers** (or comparable powered monitors) so each station is audible in the ensemble without a house system.
